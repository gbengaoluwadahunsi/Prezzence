Prezzence Account & Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your Prezzence account and associated data, you can delete your account directly in the app or by email request.

Method 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Prezzence app on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account, sessions, scores, and stored data will be removed.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

Send an email to support@prezzence.app with the subject Account Deletion Request from your registered email address. We will process your request within 48 hours.
